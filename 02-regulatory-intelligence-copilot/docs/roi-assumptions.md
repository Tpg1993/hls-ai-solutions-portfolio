# ROI Calculations & Financial Assumptions: Regulatory Intelligence

## 1. Industry Context

- **Cost of Submission Delay**: Delaying a major drug filing (NDA/BLA) costs biopharma companies $1M to $5M per month in lost exclusivity.
- **Regulatory Guidance Volume**: FDA and EMA publish hundreds of guidance updates, concept papers, and Q&A documents annually.
- **Manual Review Burden**: Regulatory affairs teams spend 40–80 hours per major guidance release manually reviewing diffs, interpreting implications, and updating submission dossiers.

---

## 2. ROI Parameters & Cost Savings

| Metric | Baseline (Manual) | With Copilot | Net Improvement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Guidance Review Time** | 50 hours / guidance | 5 hours / guidance | **90% time savings** |
| **Cross-Version Diff Analysis** | 24 hours / release | 15 minutes | **98% time savings** |
| **Omission / Refusal-to-File Risk**| 3.2% risk of filing gap | < 0.1% | **3.1% absolute risk reduction** |

---

## 3. Financial Model for a Mid-Size Pharma (15 Programs)

$$\text{Annual Net Value} = (\text{Regulatory Hours Saved} \times \$140/\text{hr}) + \text{Avoidance of 1 Refusal-to-File (RTF) Delay}$$

- **Direct Labor Savings**: 40 guidance reviews/yr $\times$ 45 hours saved $\times$ $140/hour = **$252,000**.
- **Avoidance of 1 RTF Delay (3 Months)**: 3 months $\times$ $2,000,000/month = **$6,000,000**.
- **Total Annual Economic Value**: **~$6.25 Million**.
