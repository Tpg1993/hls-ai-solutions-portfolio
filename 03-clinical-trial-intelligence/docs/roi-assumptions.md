# ROI Calculations & Financial Business Assumptions

## 1. Industry Context & Cost of Delay

In biopharmaceutical clinical trials:
- **Daily Trial Overhead**: $600,000 to $8,000,000 per day in patent expiration loss and operational cost for Phase III blockbuster oncology assets.
- **Trial Delay Cause**: 80% of trials fail to recruit on schedule; 50% of trial sites recruit 1 or zero patients.
- **Screening Burden**: Clinical Research Coordinators (CRCs) spend 45–90 minutes per patient manually reviewing medical charts to verify protocol criteria.

## 3. Financial Impact Formula for a 40-Site Phase III Trial

$$\text{Annual Net Value} = (\text{CRC Hours Saved} \times \text{Loaded Rate}) + (\text{Sites Rescued} \times \$800,000) + (\text{Months of Patent Acceleration} \times \$2,500,000)$$

- **Labor Savings**: 500 screened patients/year $\times$ 0.9 hours saved $\times$ $110/hour = **$49,500**.
- **Site Rescue Avoidance**: 2 high-risk sites rescued proactively before failing = **$1,600,000**.
- **Study Acceleration**: Average 1.5 months reduction in recruitment duration $\times$ $2,500,000/month = **$3,750,000**.
- **Total Estimated Impact**: **~$5.4 Million** in study lifecycle savings per Phase III trial.
