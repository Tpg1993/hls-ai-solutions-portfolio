# ROI Calculations & Financial Assumptions: Pharmacovigilance

## 1. Operational Industry Context

- **Case Intake Bottleneck**: Drug safety departments at mid-to-large biopharma receive 50,000 to 500,000 spontaneous reports per year.
- **Manual Effort**: Safety specialists spend 45–60 minutes per individual case manually transcribing narrative text, searching dictionaries, and redacting patient privacy identifiers.
- **Late Reporting Penalties**: Regulatory authorities (FDA, EMA) issue warning letters and multimillion-dollar penalties for failure to report serious unexpected adverse reactions within 15 days.

## 3. Financial Impact for 25,000 Cases / Year

$$\text{Annual Net Value} = (\text{Cases Processed} \times 0.58 \text{ Hours Saved} \times \$95/\text{hr}) + \text{Penalty Avoidance}$$

- **Labor Savings**: $25,000 \times 0.58 \times \$95$ = **$1,377,500 / year**.
- **Regulatory Penalty & Audit Finding Avoidance**: Estimated **$1,500,000**.
- **Total Annual Economic Return**: **~$2.87 Million**.
